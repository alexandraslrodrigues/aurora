Entorhinal cortical atrophy (ERICA) score

Visual rating of entorhinal cortex atrophy on coronal MRI (Enkirch score): 0–3 per side.

Open the calculator

Definition and grades

Orientation: coronal sections aligned to the brainstem with a section thickness of 1 mm.

  • 0: normal volume of the entorhinal cortex and parahippocampal gyrus.
  • 1: mild atrophy with widening of the collateral sulcus.
  • 2: moderate atrophy with detachment of the entorhinal cortex from the cerebellar tentorium (the 'tentorial cleft sign').
  • 3: pronounced atrophy of the parahippocampal gyrus and a wide cleft between entorhinal cortex and the cerebellar tentorium.

Published cut-off (each side): ≥2.

Figure adapted from: Roberge, X., Brisson, M. & Laforce, R. J. (2023). Specificity of Entorhinal Atrophy MRI Scale in Predicting Alzheimer’s Disease Conversion. Canadian Journal of Neurological Sciences, 50, 112–114. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Scale adapted from: Enkirch, S. J. et al. (2018). The ERICA Score: An MR Imaging–based Visual Scoring System for the Assessment of Entorhinal Cortex Atrophy in Alzheimer Disease. Radiology, 288(1), 226–233.

In the structured report

The same scale is part of the Aurora Report for cognitive disorders, which adds the result to a structured report in English or Portuguese.

About Aurora

Aurora gathers published scales and cutoffs to support structured reporting. It is not a certified medical device and does not give a diagnosis.

Aurora is described in: Rodrigues ASL, Lobo GG, Machado T, Pereira DJ. Aurora: Open-Access Web Application for Structured Neuroradiology Report and Score Calculation. AJNR Am J Neuroradiol 2026;47(2):419–423. doi:10.3174/ajnr.A8940.
