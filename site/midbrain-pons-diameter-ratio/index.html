Midbrain/pons diameter ratio calculator

A PSP marker: it falls when the midbrain is atrophic relative to the pons.

Open the calculator

Formula

  • Midbrain anteroposterior diameter: measured on the midsagittal T1-weighted image
  • Midbrain/pons diameter ratio = midbrain diameter / pontine base diameter

Measurements needed:

  • Midbrain AP diameter, collicular plate excluded (mm)
  • Pontine base AP diameter, tegmentum excluded (mm)

Above 80 years, the reliability of published thresholds is uncertain: age-related midbrain atrophy can reduce specificity (Chougar 2024). MRPI is less affected by age than the midbrain/pons area ratio (Morelli 2014).

Published cutoffs depend on method, field strength and cohort. They support imaging assessment but are not diagnostic in isolation.

How to measure

Midbrain and pons anteroposterior diameters

  1. Midsagittal T1-weighted image.
  2. Place an elliptical region over the midbrain and another over the pons.
  3. Draw the long (oblique superior–inferior) axis of each ellipse, then measure the maximal diameter perpendicular to that axis.
  4. The midbrain measurement excludes the collicular plate; the pons measurement is the pontine base (pontine tegmentum excluded).

Method: Massey 2013.

Abbreviations: PSP, progressive supranuclear palsy; PD, Parkinson’s disease; MSA, multiple system atrophy; T, tesla.

About Aurora

Aurora gathers published scales and cutoffs to support structured reporting. It is not a certified medical device and does not give a diagnosis.
