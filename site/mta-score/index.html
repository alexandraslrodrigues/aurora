Medial temporal atrophy (MTA) score

Visual rating of medial temporal lobe atrophy on coronal T1-weighted MRI (Scheltens score): 0–4 per side, with age-adjusted cut-offs.

Open the calculator

Definition and grades

Medial Temporal Lobe Atrophy (MTA) score in coronal T1-weighted MRI. The score is based on qualitative evaluation of the widening of the choroid fissure, enlargement of the temporal horn, and atrophy of the hippocampus.

  • 0: no CSF is visible around the hippocampus
  • 1: choroid fissure is slightly widened
  • 2: moderate widening of the choroid fissure, mild enlargement of the temporal horn and mild loss of hippocampal height
  • 3: marked widening of the choroid fissure, moderate enlargement of the temporal horn, and moderate loss of hippocampal height
  • 4: marked widening of the choroid fissure, marked enlargement of the temporal horn, and the hippocampus is markedly atrophied and internal structure is lost

Published age-adjusted cut-offs (each hemisphere): ≥2 under 75 years; ≥3 from 75 years.

Figure adapted from: Håkansson, C. et al. (2022). Inter-modality assessment of medial temporal lobe atrophy in a non-demented population: application of a visual rating scale template across radiologists with varying clinical experience. Eur Radiol, 32, 1127–1134. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.

MTA scale adapted from: Scheltens, P., Leys, D., Barkhof, F. et al. (1992). Atrophy of medial temporal lobes on MRI in “probable” Alzheimer’s disease and normal ageing: diagnostic value and neuropsychological correlates. Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery, and Psychiatry, 55(10), 967–972.

Reliability: Scheltens, P., Launer, L., Barkhof, F. et al. (1995). Visual assessment of medial temporal lobe atrophy on magnetic resonance imaging: Interobserver reliability. Journal of Neurology, 242(9), 557–560.

Age-adjusted cut-off: Torisson, G., van Westen, D., Stavenow, L., Minthon, L. & Londos, E. (2015). Medial temporal lobe atrophy is underreported and may have important clinical correlates in medical inpatients. BMC Geriatrics, 15, 65.

In the structured report

The same scale is part of the Aurora Report for cognitive disorders, which adds the result to a structured report in English or Portuguese.

About Aurora

Aurora gathers published scales and cutoffs to support structured reporting. It is not a certified medical device and does not give a diagnosis.

Aurora is described in: Rodrigues ASL, Lobo GG, Machado T, Pereira DJ. Aurora: Open-Access Web Application for Structured Neuroradiology Report and Score Calculation. AJNR Am J Neuroradiol 2026;47(2):419–423. doi:10.3174/ajnr.A8940.
