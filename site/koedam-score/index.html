Posterior parietal atrophy (Koedam) score

Visual rating of posterior parietal atrophy on brain MRI (Koedam score): 0–3 per side.

Open the calculator

Definition and grades

Orientation: sagittal, axial and coronal images.

Assess: on sagittal images, the posterior cingulate sulcus, the parieto-occipital sulcus and atrophy of the precuneus; on axial and coronal images, widening of the posterior cingulate sulcus and sulcal dilatation in the parietal lobes.

Scale: 0 = no atrophy; 1 = mild; 2 = moderate; 3 = severe.

Adapted from: Koedam, E. L. G. E. et al. (2011). Visual assessment of posterior atrophy: development of a MRI rating scale. European Radiology, 21, 2618–2625. Licensed under CC BY-NC.

In the structured report

The same scale is part of the Aurora Report for cognitive disorders, which adds the result to a structured report in English or Portuguese.

About Aurora

Aurora gathers published scales and cutoffs to support structured reporting. It is not a certified medical device and does not give a diagnosis.

Aurora is described in: Rodrigues ASL, Lobo GG, Machado T, Pereira DJ. Aurora: Open-Access Web Application for Structured Neuroradiology Report and Score Calculation. AJNR Am J Neuroradiol 2026;47(2):419–423. doi:10.3174/ajnr.A8940.
