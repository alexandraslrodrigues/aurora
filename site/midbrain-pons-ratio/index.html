Midbrain/pons area ratio calculator

A PSP marker: it falls when the midbrain is atrophic relative to the pons. Values below the cutoff are associated with PSP.

Open the calculator

Formula

Midbrain/pons area ratio = midbrain area / pons area

Measurements needed:

  • Midbrain surface area (mm²)
  • Pons surface area (mm²)

Published cutoffs

CutoffReported forSource
< 0.18PSP vs PD and MSAReference cutoff in Aurora; 1.5 T; sensitivity 81.4%, specificity 87.1%Mangesius 2018
< 0.22PSPProposed from the literature, not tested in a cohortPeralta 2022; Chougar 2024

Above 80 years, the reliability of published thresholds is uncertain: age-related midbrain atrophy can reduce specificity (Chougar 2024). MRPI is less affected by age than the midbrain/pons area ratio (Morelli 2014).

Published cutoffs depend on method, field strength and cohort. They support imaging assessment but are not diagnostic in isolation.

How to measure

Midbrain and pons areas

  1. True midsagittal T1-weighted image (through the aqueduct and the interpeduncular cistern).
  2. Line A: through the superior pontine notch and the inferior edge of the quadrigeminal plate.
  3. Line B: parallel to line A, through the inferior pontine notch.
  4. Midbrain area: the delta-shaped midbrain tegmentum above line A, closed by line A; the tectum (quadrigeminal plate) is excluded.
  5. Pons area: between lines A and B, bounded by the anterior and posterior margins of the pons.

Method: Quattrone 2008; Oba 2005.

Abbreviations: PSP, progressive supranuclear palsy; PD, Parkinson’s disease; MSA, multiple system atrophy; T, tesla.

About Aurora

Aurora gathers published scales and cutoffs to support structured reporting. It is not a certified medical device and does not give a diagnosis.

Aurora is described in: Rodrigues ASL, Lobo GG, Machado T, Pereira DJ. Aurora: Open-Access Web Application for Structured Neuroradiology Report and Score Calculation. AJNR Am J Neuroradiol 2026;47(2):419–423. doi:10.3174/ajnr.A8940.
