Aurora: structured neuroradiology reporting for cognitive and movement disorders

Aurora is a free, open-access web tool for structured, reproducible brain MRI reports. It brings together published visual rating scales, calculators, recommended MRI protocols and reference images, each linked to its peer-reviewed source. Reports can be written in English (US) or Portuguese (Portugal).

Cognitive disorders

A structured report of small vessel disease (white matter hyperintensities with the Fazekas scale, lacunes, recent small subcortical infarcts, microbleeds, cortical superficial siderosis, perivascular spaces, total SVD score) and brain atrophy, with copy and Excel export. Calculators for global cortical atrophy (GCA), medial temporal atrophy (MTA, with age-adjusted cut-offs), entorhinal cortical atrophy (ERICA) and posterior parietal atrophy (Koedam). A recommended MRI protocol for a suspected cognitive disorder.

Movement disorders

Calculators for the midbrain/pons area ratio, MRPI, MRPI 2.0, the midbrain/pons diameter ratio and middle cerebellar peduncle width, each with the published cut-offs and their sources. A recommended MRI protocol for suspected parkinsonism.

Evidence and limits

Aurora is described in Rodrigues ASL, Lobo GG, Machado T, Pereira DJ. Aurora: Open-Access Web Application for Structured Neuroradiology Report and Score Calculation. AJNR Am J Neuroradiol 2026;47(2):419–423. doi:10.3174/ajnr.A8940. It is a reporting aid, not a certified medical device, and does not make diagnoses. What you enter stays in your browser.
