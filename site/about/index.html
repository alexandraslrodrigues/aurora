Structured neuroradiology reporting, built on published evidence

Aurora brings validated visual scales, calculators and reference images together in one place, so that brain MRI findings are reported systematically and reproducibly. It covers cognitive and movement disorders, with more modules in development.

Principles

Built on published evidence
Every scale, cutoff and reference image comes from peer-reviewed literature, with a link to the original source.
A reporting aid, not a diagnosis
Aurora is not a certified medical device and does not make diagnoses. Interpretation stays with the radiologist.
Runs on your device
Everything works in your browser, and nothing you enter leaves your device.
Free and openly licensed
Aurora is free to use, and its code is on GitHub (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
