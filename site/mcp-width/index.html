Middle cerebellar peduncle (MCP) width

An MSA marker: narrowing indicates middle cerebellar peduncle atrophy, an MRI marker in the MDS 2022 MSA criteria; the criteria use visual assessment and do not specify the quantitative cutoff used here.

Open the calculator

Formula

Middle cerebellar peduncle width = mean of the right and left peduncles

Measurements needed:

  • Width of middle cerebellar peduncles (mm)

Published cutoffs

CutoffSuggestsSource
≤ 8.0 mmMSA rather than PDReference cutoff in Aurora; 1.5 T; 16 MSA vs 26 PD; sensitivity and specificity 100%Nicoletti 2006
< 8.0 mmMSA rather than PD and PSP1.5 T; sensitivity 69.3%, specificity 88.8%Mangesius 2018

Published cutoffs depend on method, field strength and cohort. They support imaging assessment but are not diagnostic in isolation.

How to measure

Middle cerebellar peduncle (MCP) width

  1. Parasagittal T1-weighted image that best shows the MCP between the pons and the cerebellum.
  2. Measure the distance between the superior and inferior borders of the MCP, delimited by the cerebrospinal fluid of the pontocerebellar cisterns.
  3. Measure left and right, and enter the mean.

Method: Quattrone 2008; Nicoletti 2006.

Abbreviations: PSP, progressive supranuclear palsy; PD, Parkinson’s disease; MSA, multiple system atrophy; MCP, middle cerebellar peduncle; MDS, International Parkinson and Movement Disorder Society; MRI, magnetic resonance imaging; T, tesla.

About Aurora

Aurora gathers published scales and cutoffs to support structured reporting. It is not a certified medical device and does not give a diagnosis.
